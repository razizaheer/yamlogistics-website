WHAT WE DO FOR AGENTS

Everything after the ship arrives.

PRE-ALERT Arrival handling Pre-alerts checked, consignees contacted and documents chased before the vessel berths.
IGM Manifest & CFS IGM and line filings checked, CFS nominated and container movement arranged.
DELIVERY ORDER DO release Delivery orders issued against your house bills of lading or air waybills, with local charges collected.
CLEARANCE Customs for your consignee Bill of entry, duty and out of charge, under our own customs licence.
DELIVERY Door delivery Trucking to the consignee's door anywhere in India, with proof of delivery.
EXPORT Export bookings Your nominated export cargo from India: booking, clearance and loading.
HOW IT WORKS

From pre-alert to proof of delivery.

1 Pre-alert HBL or HAWB and arrival notice received.
2 Contact Consignee informed and documents collected.
3 Arrival IGM checked, CFS and container located.
4 Release Delivery order issued, local charges settled.
5 Clear Customs clearance, if the consignee wants it.
6 Deliver Delivered, and your account settled monthly.

Agency questions

Do you work with WCA or other network members?+

We work with independent forwarders worldwide, inside and outside networks. Many partnerships go back decades.

How do you settle accounts with agents?+

We agree the arrangement upfront, usually a monthly statement of profit share or handling fees, with every invoice and receipt available.

Can you handle FCL and LCL arrivals?+

Yes, both, at the main container ports, ICDs and airports.

Can you also handle the customs clearance?+

Yes. Unlike a plain DO agent, we hold our own customs licence, so the consignee can clear with us too.
