FAQ

Air Côte d'Ivoire cargo tracking questions

What is the Air Côte d'Ivoire AWB prefix?+

Air Côte d'Ivoire air waybills start with the 3-digit prefix 483 (IATA airline code HF).

How do I track a Air Côte d'Ivoire air waybill?+

Paste the 11-digit AWB, for example 483-1807 5772, into the free tracker on this page and press Track. Dashes and spaces do not matter.

How is the Air Côte d'Ivoire AWB check digit worked out?+

The last digit is the remainder when the 7-digit serial is divided by 7. For 483-1807 5772 the serial is 1807577, and 1807577 ÷ 7 leaves 2, so the AWB is valid.

My Air Côte d'Ivoire AWB shows no status. Why?+

New AWBs often show nothing until the cargo is accepted at the airline terminal. If it still shows nothing after acceptance, check the prefix and digits, or ask your forwarder for the booked flight.
