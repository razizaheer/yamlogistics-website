HOW IT WORKS

How to track a shipment at ICD Sabarmati

1Track the vessel legPaste the container number above to see the shipping line’s live status and ETA at ICD Sabarmati.
2Follow the India legUse the Logistics Data Bank button for port gate, CFS, ICD and rail movements in India.
3Clear customsThe bill of entry or shipping bill is filed on ICEGATE, duty is paid, and customs gives out of charge (imports) or LEO (exports).
4Delivery order & gate-outThe shipping line issues the delivery order, and the container is released from the terminal or CFS.
FAQ

ICD Sabarmati tracking questions

What is the UN/LOCODE for ICD Sabarmati?+

The UN/LOCODE for ICD Sabarmati is INSBI6. You will see it on bills of lading, bills of entry and shipping bills.

How do I track a container at ICD Sabarmati?+

Paste the container number into the tracker on this page. It opens the shipping line’s live status, and the Logistics Data Bank button shows the India leg through the port, CFS, ICD and rail.

What is the Logistics Data Bank (LDB)?+

LDB is a Government of India service that tracks containers by RFID through major ports, CFSs, ICDs and rail. It is free to search by container number.

Where can I see the customs status of my bill of entry or shipping bill?+

Customs status is on ICEGATE. If YAM is your customs broker, WhatsApp us the job or container number and we will tell you the exact stage.

Can YAM clear cargo at ICD Sabarmati?+

YAM Logistics is a licensed customs broker (licence R-7/92) and has cleared cargo since 1992. WhatsApp us to arrange clearance here.
