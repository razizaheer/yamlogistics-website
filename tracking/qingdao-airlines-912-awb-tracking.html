FAQ

Qingdao Airlines cargo tracking questions

What is the Qingdao Airlines AWB prefix?+

Qingdao Airlines air waybills start with the 3-digit prefix 912 (IATA airline code QW).

How do I track a Qingdao Airlines air waybill?+

Paste the 11-digit AWB, for example 912-6964 3044, into the free tracker on this page and press Track. Dashes and spaces do not matter.

How is the Qingdao Airlines AWB check digit worked out?+

The last digit is the remainder when the 7-digit serial is divided by 7. For 912-6964 3044 the serial is 6964304, and 6964304 ÷ 7 leaves 4, so the AWB is valid.

My Qingdao Airlines AWB shows no status. Why?+

New AWBs often show nothing until the cargo is accepted at the airline terminal. If it still shows nothing after acceptance, check the prefix and digits, or ask your forwarder for the booked flight.
