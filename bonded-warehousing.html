BONDED WAREHOUSING · FTWZ · MANUFACTURING IN BOND

Pay duty when you sell, not when it lands.

Import in bulk, store under customs bond and clear it lot by lot as your buyers order. Or never pay Indian duty at all, if the goods go on to another country. We run the filings, the bond and the paperwork from arrival to final release.

WHY STORE IN BOND

Duty deferred. Cash freed. Options kept open.

CASH FLOW Defer import duty Duty is paid only when each lot is cleared for sale in India, not when the ship arrives.
LOTS Clear as you sell Bring in one large consignment and release it in smaller lots as orders come in.
RE-EXPORT Zero Indian duty on re-export Goods sent on to another country from bond pay no Indian import duty at all.
VALUE ADD Sort, repack, relabel Owners can inspect, sort, repack and relabel goods in the warehouse with customs permission.
MANUFACTURING Manufacture in bond (MOOWR) Make products inside a bonded facility under Section 65, with duty deferred on imported inputs.
SECURITY Customs-supervised storage Goods stay under customs control the whole time, with every movement recorded.
HOW A BONDED IMPORT WORKS

In bond, out in lots, or out of India.

1 Choose the facility Public or private bonded warehouse, or an FTWZ, matched to your goods and timeline.
2 Into-bond bill of entry Filed on arrival for warehousing instead of home consumption. No duty paid yet.
3 Warehousing bond Bond executed under Section 59 and goods deposited in the warehouse.
4 Store & add value Goods held, inspected, sorted or relabelled under customs supervision.
5 Ex-bond clearance Each lot cleared for India with duty paid on that lot only, under Section 68.
6 Or re-export Shipped to a buyer abroad under Section 69, with no Indian import duty.

Bonded warehouse or FTWZ?

Both let you store imported goods without paying duty upfront. Which one fits depends on how long you hold the goods and what you do with them.

Bonded warehousing questions

What is a customs bonded warehouse?+

A warehouse licensed by Indian Customs where imported goods can be stored without paying duty. Duty becomes payable only when goods are cleared for use in India. If they are exported instead, no Indian import duty is paid.

How long can goods stay in bond?+

It depends on the type of goods and the warehouse. Extensions are possible in many cases. We check the rules that apply to your goods before they arrive.

Can I clear part of a shipment?+

Yes. That is one of the main reasons to use a bonded warehouse. Each lot is cleared on its own ex-bond bill of entry, with duty paid only on that lot.

Do you have your own bonded warehouse?+

We work with licensed public bonded warehouses and FTWZs at the major ports and handle all the customs filings ourselves, as your licensed broker.

What is MOOWR?+

The Manufacture and Other Operations in Warehouse Regulations under Section 65. They let a licensed facility manufacture with imported inputs while the duty stays deferred.
