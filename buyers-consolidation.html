WHO IT’S FOR

Importers who buy from India again and again.

Retailers, wholesalers, brands and sourcing agents who place orders with several Indian suppliers at once: rugs from one town, brassware from another, textiles from a third. Each order alone is too small for its own container, and chasing ten factories from abroad takes weeks of emails and calls.

We do the chasing, the collecting and the shipping. You get one weekly report, one shipment and one set of documents.

Consolidation questions

What is buyer’s consolidation?+

Combining orders from several suppliers into a single shipment for one buyer. Instead of many small part-load shipments, you receive one container or one air shipment, with lower freight per unit and a single clearance at your end.

Which suppliers can you collect from?+

Suppliers anywhere in India. We arrange trucks from factories and workshops to a consolidation hub near Nhava Sheva, Mundra or Delhi NCR, or near the airport for air shipments.

Do you check quality?+

We count, measure and photograph every carton on arrival and check shipping marks against your instructions. For full product quality inspection, we can arrange an independent inspection agency at the supplier or at our hub.

What if one supplier is late?+

We tell you as soon as we know, with options: wait, ship the rest now and send the late order later, or move it to air. You decide.

Do you pay the suppliers?+

No. You keep paying your suppliers directly as you do today. We manage the follow-up, logistics and paperwork.

Can you consolidate air shipments too?+

Yes. Smaller or urgent orders from several suppliers can be combined into one air shipment under one master air waybill.
