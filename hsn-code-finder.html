Four questions that decide the code

What is it made of?

Material decides the chapter for most goods: a cotton shirt and a polyester shirt have different codes. Include the main material in your search.

What does it do?

Machines and electronics are classified by their function. Search for what it does (“pump”, “charger”, “router”) rather than a brand or model name.

Complete item or a part?

Parts often have their own lines, usually ending in “parts”. If you ship parts, add the word “parts” to your search.

Knitted or woven?

Clothing is split between Chapter 61 (knitted) and Chapter 62 (woven). A T-shirt is usually knitted; a formal shirt is usually woven.

Questions people ask

What is an HSN code?+

HSN (Harmonized System of Nomenclature) is the international system for classifying goods. The first 6 digits are the same worldwide; India adds 2 more to make the 8-digit ITC(HS) or Customs Tariff code used on bills of entry, shipping bills and GST invoices.

Is the HSN code the same for import, export and GST?+

The 8-digit code is the same in the Customs Tariff and ITC(HS) for import and export. GST invoices usually show 4, 6 or 8 digits depending on turnover, but they follow the same structure.

Why do I get several possible codes?+

Many products can sound alike in words but fall in different chapters depending on material, function or whether they are parts. The finder shows every line that matches your words; the right one is the line whose full description, read with the chapter notes, fits your goods.

Can I rely on the code the finder suggests?+

No. It is a starting point for guidance only. The code you declare is your responsibility, and a wrong code can cost you duty, penalties or export benefits. Ask a licensed customs broker to confirm, or apply for an advance ruling if the case is unclear.

What does Restricted or Prohibited mean?+

This is the ITC(HS) import or export policy for the code. Free means no licence is needed under that policy. Restricted needs a DGFT authorisation or must meet conditions. Prohibited cannot be imported or exported. State trading means only notified state agencies may trade it.

Where does the data come from?+

The Indian Customs Tariff (First Schedule to the Customs Tariff Act, 1975, as amended, including Budget 2026 changes) and the DGFT ITC(HS) 2022 import and export policy schedules, as on the date shown under the results.
