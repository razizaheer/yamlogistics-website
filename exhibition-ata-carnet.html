HOW AN EXHIBITION SHIPMENT WORKS

Planned back from opening day.

1 Tell us the show Dates, hall and a list of what you are bringing.
2 Choose the route ATA carnet, or temporary import against bond. We advise which suits your goods.
3 Ship & clear Air or sea to India, cleared duty-free for the exhibition.
4 To your stand Delivered to the hall during build-up, empty cases stored.
5 After the show Repacked, any sold items regularised, and the rest prepared for return.
6 Back home Re-exported, carnet vouchers or bond closed, and confirmation sent to you.

Exhibition & carnet questions

What is an ATA carnet?+

An international customs document, often called a passport for goods, that lets exhibition goods, commercial samples and professional equipment enter a country temporarily without paying duty. In India, carnets are issued and guaranteed by FICCI.

What if I don’t have a carnet?+

Exhibition goods can usually still be imported temporarily without duty against a bond and an undertaking to re-export them within the allowed period. We handle the bond and the paperwork.

What happens if I sell exhibits at the show?+

Goods sold or given away in India become a regular import. We clear them with the applicable duty paid and re-export the rest.

Can you deliver straight to the hall?+

Yes. We coordinate with the venue and organiser’s official contractor so your crates reach the stand during build-up, and we collect them after the show.

How early should I contact you?+

Ideally six to eight weeks before the show for sea freight, and three to four weeks for air. Last-minute shipments are possible, but cost more.
