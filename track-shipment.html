Tracking questions

Which numbers can I track with?+

Your YAM job number, air waybill (AWB), container number, bill of lading, shipping bill or bill of entry number. Any one is enough.

How do I track an air waybill (AWB)?+

Enter the 11-digit AWB: the 3-digit airline prefix followed by the 8-digit serial, for example 123-4567 8905. You will see flight status plus the export or import customs steps.

How do I track a container?+

Enter the 11-character container number: 4 letters and 7 digits, for example SMPU 771234 9. You will see vessel milestones, port arrival, customs and delivery.

What do LEO and out of charge mean?+

LEO (Let Export Order) is customs permission for export cargo to leave India. Out of charge is customs permission for import cargo to be taken out of the port or CFS. Until you see these, cargo cannot move.

Can I get updates without checking the website?+

Yes. Turn on WhatsApp or email alerts and we will message you at each milestone, including customs steps.
