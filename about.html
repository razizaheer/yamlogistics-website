FROM THE MANAGING DIRECTOR

Long before I understood logistics, I understood what a commitment meant.

When my father, Zaheer Zaidi, opened a small office on Mall Road, Kanpur in 1962, he wasn't trying to build one of India's oldest logistics companies. He simply believed that a person's word should always mean something.

Since then our industry has gone from telex machines and handwritten documents to faceless assessment and real-time tracking. The tools have changed. The values haven't. Many of our colleagues have spent their whole careers here, some of our clients have been with us for generations, and most new business still comes from people who already trust us.

My job is to keep it that way, and to prepare YAM for the next generation.

“For us, every shipment is important. Every relationship is priceless.”
Razi Zaheer · Managing Director, YAM Logistics Pvt. Ltd.

OUR GLOBAL PARTNERS

Before networks, there were friendships.

Long before international forwarding networks connected the world, we had built our own, one relationship at a time. For nearly six decades we have worked with independent forwarders and customs brokers in every major trading nation. Many are family businesses, now led by the second and third generations, just like ours.

When your shipment reaches another country, it is in the hands of someone we have known and trusted for decades.
