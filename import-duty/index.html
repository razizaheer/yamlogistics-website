QUESTIONS

Good to know.

How is import duty calculated in India?

Basic customs duty is charged on the assessable (CIF) value. Social welfare surcharge is 10% of the basic duty unless exempt. AIDC applies to some goods. IGST is then charged on the value plus all those duties.

What is the difference between an HS code and an HSN code?

The first six digits are the international HS code. India adds two more digits to make the eight-digit HSN (ITC-HS) code, and the duty rate is set at eight digits.

Which products are listed here?

The 1,000 six-digit HS subheadings that India imported from the largest number of countries in 2025. For any other product, use the customs duty calculator.

Are these rates final?

No. They are the standard rates in our data and are for guidance. Classification, exemptions, trade agreements and anti-dumping duty can change the amount. Verify with Customs and a licensed customs broker.
