HOW TO PRINT

Right size, first time.

1 Pick the PDF Each PDF is an A4 sheet with one label at its exact regulation size.
2 Print at 100% In the print dialog choose “Actual size” or 100% scale, never “Fit to page”.
3 Measure once Check the printed label against the minimum size on the card, e.g. 100 × 100 mm for the battery mark.
4 Fill in and stick Write in UN numbers or names where shown. Use durable, weather-resistant label stock for real shipments.
Please note. This artwork follows the label figures in the UN Model Regulations and the ICAO Technical Instructions, which the IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations (DGR) use. It is provided free for convenience and is not an official IATA product. Always check labels against the current DGR edition and any state or operator variations. The shipper is responsible for correct classification, packing, marking and labelling.

Label questions

Which labels does a mobile phone shipment need?+

Most phone exports ship as UN3481, lithium-ion batteries contained in equipment, under Section II of Packing Instruction 967. These need the battery mark showing UN3481. Fully regulated (Section I) shipments need the Class 9 lithium battery label instead, and may need Cargo Aircraft Only. Our team confirms this for every shipment.

Why is there no phone number on the battery mark?+

The battery mark no longer needs a telephone number. From 1 January 2027 the mark may not show one, so all our versions use the current design without it.

Can I print these on a normal office printer?+

Yes, for one-off or sample shipments. For regular shipping, print the PNG or SVG on durable self-adhesive label stock with a thermal or colour label printer. Colours must match the label design, and labels must stay legible for the whole journey.

Are these official IATA labels?+

No. They are free artwork drawn to the figures in the UN Model Regulations and ICAO Technical Instructions, which the IATA DGR also uses. Labels do not have to be bought from IATA, but they must match the regulation figures in shape, colour, symbols and minimum size.

Where do the labels go on the package?+

On the same side as the proper shipping name and UN number, next to the shipper or consignee address where possible. Do not fold labels over edges. Package orientation labels go on two opposite sides.
