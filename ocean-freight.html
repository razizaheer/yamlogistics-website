Ocean freight questions

FCL or LCL: which is cheaper for me?+

As a rule of thumb, once you are above roughly 13–15 m³, a full 20′ container often costs about the same as LCL and is faster and safer. We’ll price both and show you the comparison.

Do you handle customs at both ends?+

We clear the India end under our own licence. At origin or destination abroad we work with partner agents, coordinated by the same desk.

Can you deliver to factories near Delhi?+

Yes. We truck from Nhava Sheva and Mundra into North India, or clear at ICD Tughlakabad, Dadri, Patli, Garhi Harsaru or another NCR depot when rail suits the route better.

How quickly will I get a quote?+

A licensed broker replies within 2 working hours. If it’s urgent, WhatsApp us on @YAMLOGISTICS and you’ll usually hear back faster.
