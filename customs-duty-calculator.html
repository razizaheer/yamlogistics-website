HOW IT WORKS

From HSN code to landed cost in four steps.

1 Find your tariff line Type the 8-digit HSN code, or describe the goods in plain words. We search all 12,000+ lines of the Indian Customs Tariff and show the closest matches.
2 Choose the origin Pick the country the goods are made in. If India has a trade agreement with it, we show the preferential duty where we have it, or flag that one may apply.
3 Enter the invoice value FOB, C&F or CIF, in rupees or a foreign currency. For FOB we add 20% freight and 1.125% insurance unless you enter the actual figures to reach the assessable (CIF) value. Landing charges are no longer added, following CBIC's 2017 change to the valuation rules.
4 See duty and landed cost Basic customs duty, social welfare surcharge and IGST, the total payable, any DGFT restriction and possible exemptions, then send it to a broker to confirm.

Customs duty questions

How is Indian import duty calculated?+

Basic customs duty (BCD) is charged on the assessable value, which is the CIF value converted at the CBIC customs exchange rate. Landing charges (the old 1%) have not been added since CBIC amended the valuation rules in 2017. If freight or insurance is not known, customs practice is to add 20% of the FOB value for freight and 1.125% for insurance. Social welfare surcharge (SWS) is normally 10% of the BCD. IGST is then charged on the assessable value plus BCD and SWS. Some goods also attract agriculture cess (AIDC), compensation cess, anti-dumping or safeguard duty.

Is this calculator exact?+

It is an estimate based on the published tariff and the main notifications. The duty actually payable depends on the exact classification, exemption conditions, end use, valuation and any anti-dumping duty. A licensed broker should confirm it before you import.

Which data does it use?+

The CBIC Customs Tariff (as on 30 June 2025), the consolidated exemption Notification 45/2025-Customs, IGST Notification 9/2025-Integrated Tax (Rate), the DGFT ITC(HS) 2022 Schedule I import policy and the India–UAE CEPA Notification 22/2022 as amended. Budget 2026 changes are being added.

What does Restricted or Prohibited mean?+

Restricted goods need an import authorisation or must meet a DGFT policy condition, such as a licence, registration or certificate. Prohibited goods cannot be imported. State trading (STE) goods can only be imported through notified state agencies.

Do trade agreements reduce the duty?+

Often, yes. Goods that qualify under the rules of origin of an agreement such as India–UAE CEPA, India–ASEAN or India–Japan CEPA can pay lower or zero basic customs duty, with a valid certificate of origin. IGST is still payable.

Why does the IGST rate say 'check'?+

Some HSN codes have different GST rates depending on the exact product, packing or use. Where the rate depends on the description, we show the likely rate and the alternatives so a broker can confirm the right one.
